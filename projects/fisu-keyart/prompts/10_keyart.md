## Prompt

Wide 16:9 landscape promotional key art banner for the mobile game "Hobo
Evolution" (FisuEvolution).

STYLE - match EXACTLY the art style of the attached reference collage, which is
made of real screenshots of this game. Same 2D flat vector cartoon look, same
uniform thick black outline of constant weight, same flat colors with minimal
cel-shading, same warm cream and parchment palette with orange accents, same
big-head cartoon proportions. This is the single most important requirement:
the banner must look like it was drawn by the same hand as the reference.

CHARACTER - the hero of the banner is the scruffy street character from the
bottom-right cell of the reference: brown patched knit beanie, messy brown
hair, pale warm skin, heavy droopy half-closed eyes with dark under-eye bags,
patchy stubble beard, orange hoodie collar under a tattered blue-grey and
olive-green layered coat, holding a green glass bottle. Reproduce him
faithfully, standing full-body on the left third of the banner, feet planted,
cheeky and warm.

COMPOSITION - a left-to-right rags-to-riches progression across the banner. On
the left, the scruffy character in a grimy back alley with stacked cardboard,
graffiti on a cracked wall and cobblestones, exactly like the reference. Moving
right, the same character evolves into progressively wealthier versions of
himself: a delivery worker, an office worker in a shirt, and finally a smug
tycoon in a suit on the right, each one a little taller and better dressed, all
standing on the same ground line. Behind them the environment transitions from
the dark alley on the left to a bright sunny city street with shops and tall
buildings on the right. Golden coins arc through the air between the figures.

MOOD - funny, warm, absurd, adult-comedy. Argentine street humour. Nothing
grim, nothing photorealistic.

FORMAT - wide 16:9 landscape banner, full-bleed illustration edge to edge.
Absolutely NO text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no UI
elements, no phone frames, no buttons, no health bars. Do not include any
screenshot chrome from the reference: the reference is there for ART STYLE and
CHARACTER DESIGN only, not for its layout.
