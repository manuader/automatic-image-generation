Art style first: glossy 3D collectible vinyl toy figure render, bobblehead proportions with a huge round head and a small chunky body, smooth plastic and soft rubber materials with subtle specular highlights, soft studio lighting from the top left, gentle ambient occlusion, vivid saturated colors, playful premium mobile game look. Match EXACTLY the art style, proportions, materials and lighting of the attached reference. The attached sheet shows the protagonist at three stages of his life: draw ONLY ONE figure, the stage described below, as the same person. Football Evolution is a merge idle mobile game where one invented footballer grows from a baby into the God of Football, floor by floor. The app icon of Football Evolution: square full bleed artwork with no rounded corners and no border drawn. The protagonist as the Pro Debutant, a slim athletic nineteen year old in a plain teal football kit with a cream collar and the number 22 on the shorts, bursting toward the viewer while kicking a glossy football, big joyful grin, on a night stadium background of pitch green and floodlights with a golden light burst behind him. One strong readable shape, big head, close framing from the knees up so the bandage on his LEFT knee and his lime green boots still show. He is a footballer, not a god: no wings, no halo. No text, no letters, no watermark. He is the same invented protagonist as in the reference sheet and keeps his four fixed traits clearly visible: (1) a big upright tuft of dark brown hair sticking straight up from the crown like a cowlick, on short straight hair, never curly; (2) a sprinkle of freckles across the nose and cheeks; (3) a small cream adhesive bandage on his LEFT knee; (4) lime green football boots with cream soles. Same face shape, same brown eyes, same friendly cheeky grin as in the reference. No beard. Legal blacklist: no likeness of any real football player, no real club or national team crest, kit or color combination (no light blue and white stripes, no national team shirts), no sports brand logo, swoosh or brand stripes, no real trophy or real stadium, no number 10 anywhere.
