Match EXACTLY the art style, outline weight and color treatment of the attached reference icon - same game, same studio. The attached image is ONLY a style guide: do NOT draw its subject, draw only the new object described below.

Draw a fine illustration: a small police ticket booklet with a torn-off ticket held by a clip, a big red rubber stamp mark shape on it without letters, and a ballpoint pen.

Flat, simple illustration in the same visual language as the rest of the game: uniform thick black outline, flat colors with minimal cel-shading, no gradients, no photorealism. Centered on a plain pure white background, square image, generous margins, no text, no numbers, no letters, no watermark, no cropping.
