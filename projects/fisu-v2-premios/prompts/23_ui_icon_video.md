## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, outline weight and color treatment of the attached reference icon - same game, same studio. The attached image is ONLY a style guide: do NOT draw its subject, draw only the new object described below.

Draw a watch-video icon: a chunky rounded red play button with a white triangle, inside a thick white ring, like a sticker medallion. It must read clearly at 24 to 40 points on a phone.

Flat, simple icon in the same visual language as the rest of the game: uniform thick black outline, flat colors with minimal cel-shading, no gradients, no photorealism. Centered on a plain pure white background, square image, generous margins, no text, no letters, no watermark, no cropping.
