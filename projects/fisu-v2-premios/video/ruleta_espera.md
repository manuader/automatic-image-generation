# ruleta_espera — la Ruleta quieta (loop 4 s, fondo blanco)

Referencias: `referencias/wheel_frame.png` y `referencias/wheel_pointer.png` (el marco y la flecha de
la Ruleta del juego). Primer y último cuadro iguales.

## Prompt

A flat 2D cartoon prize wheel from a mobile game, in exactly the art style of the attached frame and pointer: thick black outline, flat colors, minimal cel-shading. A round chunky frame with small light bulbs all around its rim, the attached red pointer on top, and a short sturdy stand below. The disc inside the frame is ONE plain flat solid cream color (#F1E5C9) with no segments, no lines, no numbers and no drawings (the game draws the segments on top).

What moves: the light bulbs around the rim chase around in a loop, blinking warm yellow one after another; the pointer gives a tiny idle wiggle every second.

What stays frozen: the frame, the stand and the flat cream disc never move or rotate. Static camera, no zoom.

First and last frame identical so it loops seamlessly. Plain pure white background (#FFFFFF), no floor, no text, no hands, no characters.
