# paquete_sacude — reacción a un toque (1 s, fondo blanco)

Referencia: `referencias/pickup_package.png` como PRIMER y ÚLTIMO cuadro.

## Prompt

Animate the attached image exactly as it is: a sealed cardboard customs package with brown tape, a flat 2D cartoon object from a mobile game, thick black outline, flat colors. Same drawing, colors and position. Static camera, no zoom.

What moves: one quick, bouncy cartoon reaction as if it had just been tapped: the package jumps up a little, wobbles left-right twice with squash and stretch, the flaps bulge and the tape strains for an instant, then it settles back exactly where it started. Two or three tiny dust puffs at the base.

What stays frozen: it never actually opens, nothing comes out, no hands or characters.

Last frame identical to the first. Plain pure white background (#FFFFFF), no text.
