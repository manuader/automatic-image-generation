## Prompt

The same flat 2D cartoon prize wheel as in ruleta_espera (thick black outline, flat colors, light bulbs around the rim, the red pointer on top, a short stand, and the inner disc ONE plain flat solid cream color with nothing drawn on it). Static camera, same framing.

What moves: celebration. All the rim bulbs flash bright together three times; the pointer does a happy bounce; a ring of golden starburst rays and colorful confetti pops out from behind the frame and falls, plus a few gold four-point sparkles.

What stays frozen: the frame keeps its shape, the disc stays flat cream and does NOT rotate, nothing is drawn on the disc, no prizes, no text, no hands, no characters.

Plain flat pure chroma green background (#00FF00), perfectly uniform, no gradients and no green tones on the wheel or the confetti.
