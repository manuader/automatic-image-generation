## Prompt

Animate the attached image: a sealed cardboard customs package with brown tape, a flat 2D cartoon object from a mobile game, thick black outline, flat colors, minimal cel-shading. Same drawing, colors and position as the attached image. Static camera, no zoom, no pan.

Sequence: a short anticipation squash, then the tape snaps and the top flaps burst open upward and outward with a strong bounce and stay open; a burst of warm golden light rays shoots up out of the opening, with a few gold four-point sparkles and small glints flying up and fading; the light settles into a steady soft glow coming out of the opening for the last second.

Important: only light comes out — no objects, no money, no items, no characters and no hands come out of it (the game draws the prize on top). The object keeps the same drawing; only the part that opens moves.

Plain flat pure chroma green background (#00FF00) everywhere, perfectly uniform, no gradients, no shadows on the background, no green tones on the object or the light, no text.
