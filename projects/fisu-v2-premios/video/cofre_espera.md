# cofre_espera — el cofre esperando (loop 4 s, fondo blanco)

Referencia: `referencias/ui_chest_closed.png` como PRIMER y ÚLTIMO cuadro (loop perfecto).

## Prompt

Animate the attached image exactly as it is: a flat 2D cartoon treasure chest from a mobile game, thick black outline, flat colors, minimal cel-shading. Keep the exact same drawing, proportions, colors and position in every frame. Static camera, no zoom, no pan, no camera shake.

What moves (subtle, cozy, inviting): the chest gently breathes, rising and settling a few pixels and squashing very slightly as it lands; the gold trim catches a small glint that travels once from left to right; two tiny four-point sparkles twinkle near the lid and fade.

What stays frozen: the lid stays fully closed the whole time, the lock and straps do not move, nothing comes out of the chest, the chest never rotates or changes shape beyond the soft squash.

The first and the last frame are identical to the attached image so the clip loops seamlessly. Plain pure white background (#FFFFFF) everywhere, no floor, no shadow on the background, no text, no hands, no characters.
