# colchon_abre - se abre (3 s, fondo VERDE croma)

Referencia: `referencias/pickup_mattress.png` como PRIMER cuadro. Reemplaza al `colchon_abre` actual.

> **v2 (2026-10-12, pedido del dueño):** al abrirse tiene que salir **una explosión de dinero**: billetes y monedas
> volando por el aire desde el colchón. Los billetes NO pueden ser verdes (el fondo es verde croma y se borrarían):
> van en tonos cálidos (beige, naranja, rosado, como pesos genéricos) y celestes (como el dólar blue), más monedas doradas.

> **v3 (2026-10-13, pedido del dueño):** los billetes tienen que ser **sí o sí verdes**. Por eso este clip pasa a
> **fondo blanco** (el recorte topológico del grupo `objetos` del pipeline, `matte: blanco`), no verde: con croma verde
> los billetes se borrarían.

## Prompt

Animate the attached image: an old striped mattress, a flat 2D cartoon object from a mobile game, thick black outline, flat colors, minimal cel-shading. Same drawing, colors and position as the attached image. Static camera, no zoom, no pan.

Sequence: a short anticipation squash, then a seam along the top rips open with a big cartoon pop and a MONEY EXPLOSION bursts out of the tear: dozens of bright GREEN banknotes and shiny gold coins shoot up into the air, spin and flutter, and rain down slowly all around the mattress, with gold four-point sparkles and a burst of warm golden light rays behind them. The money keeps fluttering and falling until the end of the clip; the tear stays open with a little stuffing at its edges and a warm glow inside.

The banknotes are classic cartoon dollar bills in bright green with a darker green border and a thick black outline, generic invented money with no portraits, no words and no numbers. The coins are plain gold with a thick black outline. Every bill and coin has a clear black outline so it stands out from the background.

The mattress keeps the same drawing; only the seam opens. No hands, no characters, no text.

Plain flat pure white background (#FFFFFF) everywhere, perfectly uniform, no gradients, no floor, no shadows on the background.
