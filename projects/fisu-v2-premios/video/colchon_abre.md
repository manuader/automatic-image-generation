# colchon_abre — se abre (3 s, fondo VERDE croma)

Referencia: `referencias/pickup_mattress.png` como PRIMER cuadro. Reemplaza al `colchon_abre` actual.

## Prompt

Animate the attached image: an old striped mattress, a flat 2D cartoon object from a mobile game, thick black outline, flat colors, minimal cel-shading. Same drawing, colors and position as the attached image. Static camera, no zoom, no pan.

Sequence: a short anticipation squash, then a seam along the side rips open with a cartoon pop and the stuffing puffs out a little at the edges of the tear while the tear stays open; a burst of warm golden light rays shoots up out of the opening, with a few gold four-point sparkles and small glints flying up and fading; the light settles into a steady soft glow coming out of the opening for the last second.

Important: only light comes out — no objects, no money, no items, no characters and no hands come out of it (the game draws the prize on top). The object keeps the same drawing; only the part that opens moves.

Plain flat pure chroma green background (#00FF00) everywhere, perfectly uniform, no gradients, no shadows on the background, no green tones on the object or the light, no text.
