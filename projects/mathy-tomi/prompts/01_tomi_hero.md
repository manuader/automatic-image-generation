## Prompt

Create a NEW character in exactly the same art style as the attached reference image, which shows Lumi, the lemon teacher mascot of the math game Mathy: same thick dark outline, same glossy cel shading, same chibi proportions (big round body, tiny arms and legs), same big shiny eyes and rosy cheeks. Draw ONLY the new character: do not draw the lemon, no graduation cap.

The new character is Tomi, the lemon's best friend and helper, who gives hints when the player does not know what to do. It is Tomi, the tomato helper mascot of the math game Mathy: a round glossy tomato body, bright red with deeper red shading at the bottom and a soft pink-white highlight on the upper left, a small star-shaped crown of pointed dark green leaves with a short green stem on top, big shiny black eyes with two white highlights each, rosy pink cheeks, a wide open happy smile showing a pink tongue, short red arms with round hands, and short stubby red legs with small dark brown shoes. Its signature prop is a small yellow light bulb with a grey screw base, a thick dark outline and short solid yellow rays.

Pose: standing, cheerful and welcoming, one arm raised waving hello next to the light bulb, which floats just above that raised hand and is switched on, the other arm relaxed, slight 3/4 view facing the viewer.

Full character visible with generous margins on every side, centered on a plain pure white background, square image, no text, no letters, no watermark, no cropping. No soft glow, no halo, no sparkles and no motion lines outside the thick dark outline, so the white background touches the character's outline everywhere. The light bulb is drawn in the same style with its own thick dark outline, and its rays are short solid shapes with a dark outline, not a glow.

IMPORTANT: no white sticker border and no white outline around the character or its props: the thick dark outline is the OUTERMOST edge of everything, and the white background touches it directly. Do not leave white or light gaps between the arms, the props and the body; any gap shows the plain background.
