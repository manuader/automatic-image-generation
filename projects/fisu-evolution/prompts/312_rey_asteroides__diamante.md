## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, line weight, proportions and color treatment of the attached reference character - same game, same studio.

The attached image IS this character, already drawn and approved. Keep his face, his big-head / tiny-body cartoon proportions (the head is 55-60% of the figure), his pose and every prop EXACTLY as they are in it. This is a re-skin of an existing asset, not a new character: if the result reads as a different person, or as a realistically proportioned one, it is wrong.

Draw exactly ONE figure, centred, filling the canvas. Not a model sheet, not a turnaround, not two or three variations side by side - ONE single character, once.

Rey de los Asteroides - ALTERNATE OUTFIT "Diamond", the same asteroid king as the attached reference: his crown, his pickaxe over the shoulder, the pack on his back and the hanging scales in his raised hand all stay exactly as they are, in the same pose.

Change ONLY the material and the palette: the whole figure becomes one continuous sculpture of cut ice-blue crystal - skin, hair, every layer of clothing, every fold and every prop are the same faceted surface. The crystal is SATURATED, never pale: mid-tones in a solid ice blue around #6FA8D6, shadows stepping down to deep teal #2E5F8A and cool violet #4A4A8C in the recesses. The lightest facet in the whole figure still reads blue, never lighter than #BFE0F5, and there is no white anywhere on the figure. Highlights are small hard slivers on edges only. Same bold black outline as the reference.

Two things in the reference are simply not drawn here: the rock platform under his boots and the loose rocks floating around him. He stands with both boots directly on the empty background. The scales hang from straight open chains that touch his hand and the pans, closing no loop.

CUTOUT RULES (the white background is removed automatically):
- Nothing on the figure may be white or near-white. Pure white is the background only; no part of the character may be lighter than #BFE0F5.
- The background must never be trapped inside the drawing: no frame, no backdrop, no ground platform, no cast shadow, no closed ring or loop of rope, no hand on hip. Nothing detached from the figure: no floating debris, no free-standing sparkles.

Full body standing character, both feet planted on the ground, hands visible, complete figure with generous margins, centered on a plain pure white background, square image, no text, no watermark, no cropping.
