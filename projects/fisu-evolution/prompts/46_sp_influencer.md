## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, line weight, proportions and color treatment of the attached reference character — same game, same studio.

El Influencer is a rare special-drop character in Fisura Evolution, an Argentine merge-idle mobile game where a street survivor evolves all the way into a god. She is a social media influencer standing in a rehearsed pose with one hip cocked, holding a phone on a selfie stick high with one hand, shopping bags hooked on the other forearm. This captures the monetizes every waking second of her life. Don't drop a single one of these props, they carry the joke: phone on selfie stick, ring light glowing behind her head like a halo, glossy shopping bags on forearm, floating heart and thumbs-up symbols around her. Her expression: practiced duck-face pout.

CRITICAL — the shopping bags and her clothing must be COMPLETELY BLANK. Do not draw any brand name, any logo, any monogram, any initials, any interlocking letters, any crest and any emblem, on the bags, on the tracksuit, on the shoes or anywhere else in the image. No real-world fashion brands and no invented ones either. The bags are plain solid pastel colours with only a paper handle and a simple fold line; the tracksuit is a plain solid colour with at most a contrasting stripe down the sleeve. Her wealth reads through the NUMBER of bags and the glossy sheen, never through a label.

Full body standing character, both feet planted on the ground, hands visible, complete figure with generous margins, centered on a plain pure white background, square image, no text of any kind, no lettering, no watermark, no cropping.
