## Prompt

Vertical 9:16 mobile game promotional poster, in a bold 2D cartoon style with THICK BLACK OUTLINES and FLAT SATURATED COLOR — no airbrush gradients, no photorealism, no 3D render, no cel-shaded rendering. Match EXACTLY the line weight, the flat colour treatment, the facial style and the body proportions of the attached reference character, who is the SAME character that must appear in this poster.

Scene: the cheerful ragged hobo from the reference stands in a run-down Buenos Aires back alley at night, arms thrown up in celebration, grinning. Around him: cracked plaster walls with colourful graffiti, stacked bundles of flattened cardboard, a green dumpster, a warm street lamp pooling orange light, a clothesline of laundry overhead. A shower of golden coins bursts up from the cobblestones around his feet and arcs through the air.

Composition, measured: the TOP 30% of the frame is open sky or flat background with almost nothing in it — leave it visually calm, it is reserved. The character stands centred, feet at about 88% of the frame height, head reaching no higher than 40%.

ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT of any kind: no letters, no words, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no UI and no buttons. Never draw real-world brand names or trademarks on any object — bags, signs, clothing or packaging must be blank or carry invented marks only.
