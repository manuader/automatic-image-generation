## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, outline weight and color treatment of the attached reference icon - same game, same studio. The attached image is ONLY a style guide: do NOT draw its subject, draw only the new object described below.

The Prize Wheel of Fisura Evolution, an Argentine merge-idle mobile game where a street survivor evolves all the way into a god is presented by a TV game-show host. Draw a small complete prize wheel seen at a slight angle: colorful segments in magenta, gold, sky blue and green, a chunky gold rim studded with small round light bulbs, a gold pointer at the top and a small magenta stand at the bottom.

Flat, simple icon in the same visual language as the rest of the game: uniform thick black outline, flat colors with minimal cel-shading, no gradients, no photorealism. Centered on a plain pure white background, square image, generous margins, no text, no watermark, no cropping.
