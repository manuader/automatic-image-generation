## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, line weight, proportions and color treatment of the attached reference character - same game, same studio.

The attached image is ONLY a style guide: draw a brand-new character, not the one in the image. Keep its head-to-body ratio, its thick black outline and its flat colors with minimal cel-shading.

El Turista Gringo is a visiting character in Fisura Evolution, an Argentine merge-idle mobile game where a street survivor evolves all the way into a god: a foreign tourist who finds everything cheap and pays cash for your best employee. It is a satirical archetype, not any real person: a generic invented face, no real insignia, no real logos and no brand names.

The character: a tall lanky sunburned tourist with pink cheeks and a peeling nose, wide straw sun hat, loose basketball jersey with a big bold number 67 on the chest, khaki shorts, white socks pulled up under sandals, a camera hanging around the neck, a fanny pack and a folding paper map with no readable text. Palette: turquoise, beige and red.

Pose: standing with an excited wide grin, the folding map held open in both hands. Expression: giddy amazed grin.

Full body standing character, both feet planted on the ground, hands visible, generous margins, centered on a plain pure white background, square image, no text except the number 67 on the jersey, no watermark, no cropping.
