## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, line weight, proportions and color treatment of the attached reference character - same game, same studio.

The attached image is ONLY a style guide: draw a brand-new character, not the one in the image. Keep its head-to-body ratio, its thick black outline and its flat colors with minimal cel-shading.

El Sindicalista is a visiting character in Fisura Evolution, an Argentine merge-idle mobile game where a street survivor evolves all the way into a god: a union organizer who closes a pay deal for your workers and brings a barbecue for the neighborhood. It is a satirical archetype, not any real person: a generic invented face, no real insignia, no real logos and no brand names.

The character: a stocky union organizer with a thick neck, a short dark beard and a cloth headband tied around the forehead, padded sleeveless work vest over a plain T-shirt, jeans and work boots, carrying a megaphone and a plain blank banner on a pole with no logo and no lettering. Palette: brick red, gray and yellow.

Pose: standing proudly with the megaphone resting on one shoulder and the blank banner pole planted in the other hand. Expression: proud combative grin.

Full body standing character, both feet planted on the ground, hands visible, generous margins, centered on a plain pure white background, square image, no text, no watermark, no cropping.
