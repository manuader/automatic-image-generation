## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the brand system of the attached reference — same company, same brand. Palette, and nothing outside it: brand purple #6E4299, brand orange #F38435, near-black navy #171B25, dark slate #282B34, text grey #383838, off-white #F4F4F4, pure white. The symbol is a thick-stroked open-top letter 'U' in purple with straight vertical sides, a semicircular bottom and flat-cut stroke ends, and an orange map-pin marker — a teardrop pointing down with a small white circular hole in it — hanging inside the counter of the U. The wordmark 'URBETRACK' sits to the right of the symbol, uppercase, medium weight Montserrat, with very wide letter-spacing. Reproduce the U, the orange pin and the wordmark exactly as in the reference: never redraw them, never restyle them, never change the spelling. The signature brand graphic is a left-to-right gradient from purple #6E4299 to orange #F38435. Design a seamless repeating pattern, square 1:1, on near-black navy #171B25. The motif is the brand map-pin marker repeated across the frame at three sizes, connected by thin route lines that bend at 90 and 45 degrees like a street grid. Pins alternate between solid orange #F38435 and solid purple #6E4299; the route lines are purple at 40% opacity, one pixel thick. Small hollow circles mark the points where two routes cross. The pattern must tile: any motif touching an edge is cut and continues on the opposite side. Flat vector, no gradients, no shadows, no text at all.
