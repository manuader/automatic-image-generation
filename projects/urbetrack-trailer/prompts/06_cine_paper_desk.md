Generate an image. A photorealistic cinematic film still for a premium brand trailer, shot on a large-format cinema camera with a 35mm lens, natural and motivated light, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, rich depth with clear foreground, midground and background layers (it will be animated with a slow parallax camera move). Color grading in the Urbetrack palette: soft whites, pale lavender atmospheric haze, cool violet shadows, and warm light only where the light source motivates it; brand accents appear ONLY in Urbetrack purple (#6B3FA0) and Urbetrack orange (#F47C20). Elegant, hopeful, human and calm, never gritty or dystopian. People appear only from behind, in silhouette or softly out of focus (no recognizable faces). If the Urbetrack truck appears it is the SAME white garbage compactor truck as the attached truck image (identical design, white bodywork with the purple-to-orange swoosh stripe, amber beacon). Wide horizontal landscape image, 16:9 aspect ratio (like 1536x864), full-bleed. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT of any kind: no letters, no words, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no license plates, no street signs, no billboards, no watermark, no UI.
