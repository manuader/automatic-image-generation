## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, outline weight and color treatment of the attached reference - same game, same studio. The attached image is ONLY a style guide: do NOT copy its composition, draw only the new subject described below.

Draw a big prize bundle illustration for a rebirth offer, grouped in one compact cheerful pile: in the center, a small orange and yellow cartoon phoenix with spread wings standing on a pile of light blue dollar bundles held by paper bands; on the left, a tied burlap sack overflowing with gold coins (the normal game coins); behind on the right, a thick green zig-zag arrow pointing up; a few sparkles. The blue money is ALWAYS generic invented banknotes tinted bright light blue and turquoise, exactly like the blue bills in the attached reference, with a big bold dollar sign on the front note: no portraits, no real banknote design, no words, no serial numbers. Absolutely NO gold bars, NO gold ingots and NO golden treasure: gold must not represent this money.

Flat, simple illustration in the same visual language as the rest of the game: uniform thick black outline, flat colors with minimal cel-shading, no gradients, no photorealism. Centered on a plain pure white background, square image, generous margins, no text, no numbers, no letters, no watermark, no cropping.
