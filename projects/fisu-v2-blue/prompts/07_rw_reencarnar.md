## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, outline weight and color treatment of the attached reference - same game, same studio. The attached image is ONLY a style guide: do NOT copy its composition, draw only the new subject described below.

Draw a reincarnation hero illustration: a cartoon phoenix with spread flaming wings in orange, yellow and a touch of turquoise, rising in a swirl of light from a pile of light blue dollar bundles held by paper bands, with loose blue notes flying upward and sparkles around. Triumphant and magical. The blue money is ALWAYS generic invented banknotes tinted bright light blue and turquoise, exactly like the blue bills in the attached reference, with a big bold dollar sign on the front note: no portraits, no real banknote design, no words, no serial numbers. Absolutely NO gold bars, NO gold ingots and NO golden treasure: gold must not represent this money.

Flat, simple illustration in the same visual language as the rest of the game: uniform thick black outline, flat colors with minimal cel-shading, no gradients, no photorealism. Centered on a plain pure white background, square image, generous margins, no text, no numbers, no letters, no watermark, no cropping.
