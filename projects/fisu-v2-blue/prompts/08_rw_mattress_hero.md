Draw a hidden mattress treasure hero illustration: an old, thick, blue and white striped mattress seen in three-quarter view, bulging and puffed up because it is stuffed with money, with two sewn cloth patches, a torn seam on the side where the corners of a few green banknotes and one light blue banknote peek out, and a couple of sparkles around it hinting at a secret. Flat, simple illustration in the same visual language as the rest of the game: uniform thick black outline, flat colors with minimal cel-shading, no gradients, no photorealism. Centered on a plain pure white background, square image, generous margins, no text, no numbers, no letters, no watermark, no cropping.
