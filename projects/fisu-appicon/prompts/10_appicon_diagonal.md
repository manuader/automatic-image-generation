## Prompt

Official mobile game app icon for "Hobo Evolution" (FisuEvolution). Square 1:1.

CHARACTER - match EXACTLY the art style, line weight, proportions, colors and facial design of the attached reference image. Reproduce THIS character faithfully. Do not redesign him, do not restyle him, do not make him cuter, do not change his expression.

Head-and-shoulders portrait of "El Fisura", a scruffy but endearing Buenos Aires street character: brown patched knit beanie with a folded brim, stitched patches and small dirt spots; messy brown hair escaping at the temples; pale warm skin; heavy droopy half-closed eyes with dark under-eye bags; thick brown eyebrows; patchy stubble beard drawn with fine stipple dots; wide crooked goofy grin with the tongue lolling out to one side; bright orange hoodie collar bunched around the neck over a tattered blue-grey and olive-green layered coat, of which only the very top edge is visible at the bottom of the frame.

STYLE - 2D flat vector cartoon, single art direction. Uniform thick black outline of constant weight. Flat colors, minimal cel-shading, single soft light from the top-left. NO gradients anywhere on the character. Mobile-game production quality, adult-comedy tone.

COMPOSITION - the head is large and centered, filling about 75% of the canvas width, with the chin around the lower third and a comfortable margin above the beanie. Shoulders cropped by the bottom edge. Facing the viewer with a slight three-quarter tilt, cheeky and warm. Keep every important element at least 12% away from all four edges and corners.

BACKGROUND - a vivid sky-blue ("celeste") gradient. This is the ONLY place in the image where gradients are allowed. A smooth diagonal blend from bright pale sky blue #A8DCFF at the top-left, through saturated azure #4D96FF in the middle, to deep blue #2E6FD9 at the bottom-right. Behind the head, a soft radial glow of lighter blue, like a spotlight. Over that, a wide sunburst of low-contrast lighter-blue rays radiating outward from behind the head, and a few small flat white cartoon clouds placed near the corners, far from the face. Corners slightly darkened so the head pops forward. Energetic and dynamic, never competing with the face.

SEPARATION - draw a clean cream #FFF8E7 sticker keyline, about 2% of the canvas width, immediately outside the character black outline and following his silhouette, to lift him off the blue.

FORMAT - full-bleed artwork, edge to edge, flat opaque background, no transparency, no alpha. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark. No rounded corners, no border frame, no outer drop shadow, no device mockup, no UI elements, no App Store badge. Do not crop the head.
