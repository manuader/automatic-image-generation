## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the drawing style of the attached reference: the line weight, the flat-fill treatment, the palette and the sticker feel. Do NOT copy its subject and do NOT copy its composition — this is a different asset of the same set, not a variation of that drawing. Draw a lower-third name plate for a vlog, as flat sticker artwork. Composition in measurable terms: two stacked horizontal bars, left-aligned with each other, together filling 90% of the frame width and 30% of its height, centred in the frame. The upper bar is dark green #1B5E20 and taller, carrying the words 'SANTA TUCA' in bold rounded off-white capital letters. The lower bar is shorter, two thirds as wide, filled yellow #FFD32A, carrying the words 'CANNABIS Y CULTURA' in smaller black capital letters with wide letter spacing. A small lit tuca with a #F03A17 ember stands at the left end of the upper bar, overlapping it. Both bars have rounded corners and the same thick black outline. Centered on a plain pure white background with generous empty margins on all four sides, square image, nothing touching or cropped by the edges of the canvas, no drop shadow cast onto the background, no frame, no watermark. Every light area of the artwork itself must be off-white #E8E8E8 or a light tint of its own colour — never pure white — so the artwork stays separable from the background. No gibberish text: any letterform must be real, correctly spelled Spanish.
