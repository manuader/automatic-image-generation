## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the drawing style of the attached reference: the line weight, the flat-fill treatment, the palette and the sticker feel. Do NOT copy its subject and do NOT copy its composition — this is a different asset of the same set, not a variation of that drawing. Draw a single chunky cartoon puff of smoke, the kind used as a video transition. Composition in measurable terms: one big cloud shape fills the central 75% of the frame, built from about twelve overlapping rounded lobes of different sizes so the silhouette is bumpy and irregular, filled in off-white #E8E8E8 with #C9C9C9 flat shading along the lower left of each lobe. Three small separate puffs of the same treatment float around it — one to the upper right, two to the lower left — each no bigger than 10% of the frame. No face, no character, no text at all. Centered on a plain pure white background with generous empty margins on all four sides, square image, nothing touching or cropped by the edges of the canvas, no drop shadow cast onto the background, no frame, no watermark. Every light area of the artwork itself must be off-white #E8E8E8 or a light tint of its own colour — never pure white — so the artwork stays separable from the background.
