# 05 — Encendedor con llama

- **estado**: hecho
- **referencia**: adjuntar `output/logo_estampita.png`

## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the drawing style of the attached reference: the line weight, the flat-fill treatment, the palette and the sticker feel. Do NOT copy its subject and do NOT copy its composition — this is a different asset of the same set, not a variation of that drawing. Draw a classic disposable plastic lighter standing upright with its flame lit. Composition in measurable terms: the lighter occupies the central 45% of the frame width and 55% of its height, standing vertically, its body a rounded rectangle in green #33A02C with a #1B5E20 outline, a #7CC576 highlight stripe down its left side, a golden #E8C07A metal hood at the top and a small black thumb wheel. Rising from the hood, a teardrop-shaped flame reaching 25% of the frame height, in three flat nested tones: #F03A17 on the outside, #FFD32A in the middle and #FFE97A at the core. Four short curved spark lines around the flame. Centered on a plain pure white background with generous empty margins on all four sides, square image, nothing touching or cropped by the edges of the canvas, no drop shadow cast onto the background, no frame, no watermark. Every light area of the artwork itself must be off-white #E8E8E8 or a light tint of its own colour — never pure white — so the artwork stays separable from the background.
