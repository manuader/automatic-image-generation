# 01 — Emblema de la marca

- **estado**: hecho

## Prompt

Cartoon die-cut sticker illustration. One thick black outline of even weight (#1A1A1A) around every shape, flat fills with a single darker flat tone for shading and a single lighter flat tone for highlights. No gradients, no airbrush, no photographic texture, no 3D render, no realistic lighting. Bold, friendly, slightly chunky shapes with rounded corners — the look of a vinyl sticker or a 90s cartoon prop. Palette, and nothing outside it: outline black #1A1A1A, halo yellow #FFD32A with a soft pale-yellow glow #FFE97A, cannabis green #33A02C with dark green #1B5E20 and light green #7CC576, rolling-paper off-white #E8E8E8 with grey shadow #C9C9C9, ember orange-red #F03A17, golden tan #E8C07A with #C9954A. Draw the brand emblem of a cannabis creator called Santa Tuca — the name is Argentine slang and means 'Saint Roach', so the whole emblem is an affectionate saint parody. Composition in measurable terms: dead centre of the frame, a lit hand-rolled joint stub — a 'tuca' — stands upright, occupying 40% of the frame height, its paper off-white #E8E8E8 with #C9C9C9 shading and a glowing #F03A17 ember at the top with three tiny curls of grey smoke rising from it. Floating horizontally above the tuca, not touching it, a flat yellow #FFD32A halo ring seen in perspective as a narrow ellipse, 45% of the frame width, with a soft #FFE97A glow around its outer edge; the inside of the halo ring must be pure white. Behind the tuca, twenty straight golden #E8C07A rays radiate outward from the centre like a sunburst, reaching 80% of the frame width. Across the bottom third, a dark green #1B5E20 ribbon banner with curled ends carries the words 'SANTA TUCA' in bold rounded off-white capital letters. Centered on a plain pure white background with generous empty margins on all four sides, square image, nothing touching or cropped by the edges of the canvas, no drop shadow cast onto the background, no frame, no watermark. Every light area of the artwork itself must be off-white #E8E8E8 or a light tint of its own colour — never pure white — so the artwork stays separable from the background. No gibberish text: any letterform must be real, correctly spelled Spanish.
