## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the drawing style of the attached reference: the line weight, the flat-fill treatment, the palette and the sticker feel. Do NOT copy its subject and do NOT copy its composition — this is a different asset of the same set, not a variation of that drawing. Draw a horizontal banner plaque that tells viewers to subscribe. Across its centre, the single word 'SUSCRIBITE' in bold rounded off-white capital letters filling 70% of the plaque width. Sitting on the plaque's top-left corner and overlapping it, a serrated cannabis leaf in green #33A02C rotated about 20 degrees. Floating just above the plaque's top-right corner, a small yellow #FFD32A halo ring with a #FFE97A glow. Below the plaque, a smaller off-white #E8E8E8 tab with the word 'SANTA TUCA' in small dark green capitals. Centered on a plain pure white background with generous empty margins on all four sides, square image, nothing touching or cropped by the edges of the canvas, no drop shadow cast onto the background, no frame, no watermark. Every light area of the artwork itself must be off-white #E8E8E8 or a light tint of its own colour — never pure white — so the artwork stays separable from the background. No gibberish text: any letterform must be real, correctly spelled Spanish.
