## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the drawing style of the attached reference: the line weight, the flat-fill treatment, the palette and the sticker feel. Do NOT copy its subject and do NOT copy its composition — this is a different asset of the same set, not a variation of that drawing. Draw a classic round twin-bell alarm clock whose hands read exactly four twenty. Composition in measurable terms: the clock body fills the central 60% of the frame, a circle with a thick green #33A02C rim and an off-white #E8E8E8 face, two golden #E8C07A bells on top and two short stubby legs at the bottom. On the face, twelve black tick marks and the numerals 12, 3, 6 and 9 in bold rounded black type; the short hour hand points a little past the 4 and the long minute hand points exactly at the 4, so the time reads 4:20. A small yellow #FFD32A halo ring floats above the clock, between and just above the two bells. Three short motion lines on each side suggest the clock is ringing. Centered on a plain pure white background with generous empty margins on all four sides, square image, nothing touching or cropped by the edges of the canvas, no drop shadow cast onto the background, no frame, no watermark. Every light area of the artwork itself must be off-white #E8E8E8 or a light tint of its own colour — never pure white — so the artwork stays separable from the background. No gibberish text: any letterform must be real, correctly spelled Spanish.
