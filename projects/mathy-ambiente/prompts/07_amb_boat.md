## Prompt

A single small decorative sprite for the animated background of Mathy, a premium math puzzle game. Style: stylized 2D digital painting with clean vector-like shapes, soft gradients, dusk light with a warm golden rim light, teal and indigo tones, in the spirit of Monument Valley and Alto's Odyssey. Draw ONLY the single object described below, isolated and centered on a plain pure white background, with generous margins, square image, no text, no scenery, no ground, no cast shadow.

The object: a small origami paper boat seen from the side at a slight angle, made of light terracotta peach paper with its folds shaded in warm orange and dusk blue. No water, no reflection.

IMPORTANT for background removal: crisp clean silhouette edges; no glow, no bloom, no halo, no sparkles and no mist outside the object. The outer edge of the object must be clearly darker than white everywhere (no white or very pale colors on its border), so the white background touches a distinct edge. Do not leave holes or gaps through the object that show the white background.
