## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the brand system of the attached reference — same studio, same brand. Palette, and nothing outside it: cream background #F6EFE9, ink dark olive-grey #54574F, accent orange #EA6330, deep orange #C94D1D, muted sage grey #8B8E82. The logo is the lowercase word 'oushy' set in a rounded geometric sans (Fredoka), ink grey, with the letter 'u' in orange and an 8-pointed hand-drawn tapered star sitting over it. The 8-pointed star with slightly organic, hand-drawn tapered points is the brand symbol and must keep that exact shape. Reproduce the wordmark and the star with the exact proportions of the reference, never redraw or restyle them. Design a vertical 9:16 phone wallpaper. Composition in measurable terms: the background is a flat cream #F6EFE9 field. An enormous thin ink-grey outline 8-pointed star is centred in the frame and occupies 85% of the frame width, drawn with a 3-pixel hairline stroke and no fill, so it reads as a delicate watermark. A smaller solid orange 8-pointed star, 15% of the frame width, sits exactly at the centre of the big one. Six tiny orange small 8-pointed stars are scattered around the upper and lower thirds. The top 20% and bottom 15% of the frame are kept completely empty so the clock and icons stay readable. No text of any kind.
