## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the brand system of the attached reference — same studio, same brand. Palette, and nothing outside it: cream background #F6EFE9, ink dark olive-grey #54574F, accent orange #EA6330, deep orange #C94D1D, muted sage grey #8B8E82. The logo is the lowercase word 'oushy' set in a rounded geometric sans (Fredoka), ink grey, with the letter 'u' in orange and an 8-pointed hand-drawn tapered star sitting over it. The 8-pointed star with slightly organic, hand-drawn tapered points is the brand symbol and must keep that exact shape. Reproduce the wordmark and the star with the exact proportions of the reference, never redraw or restyle them. Editorial layout language: very generous white space, hairline rules, tiny uppercase monospaced labels with wide letter-spacing (Space Mono), large lowercase rounded headlines, and short phrases in a thin handwritten script (Homemade Apple) in orange. Design a brand key visual, square 1:1 poster. Composition in measurable terms: the cream background fills the frame; a single giant orange letter 'U' occupies the central 55% of the height, vertically centred, with the ink-grey 8-pointed star overlapping its centre exactly as in the reference lockup. The 'oushy' wordmark sits small in the top-left corner at about 18% of the frame width. Along the bottom edge, one line of tiny uppercase monospaced ink-grey text reads 'ESTUDIO CREATIVO INTEGRAL · ARG'. Three small orange stars are scattered in the empty cream areas at different sizes. Flat vector, print-quality, no photographic texture, no gradients. No watermark, no signature, no gibberish text: every letterform must be real, correctly spelled Spanish or English.
