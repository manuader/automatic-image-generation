## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the brand system of the attached reference — same studio, same brand. Palette, and nothing outside it: cream background #F6EFE9, ink dark olive-grey #54574F, accent orange #EA6330, deep orange #C94D1D, muted sage grey #8B8E82. The logo is the lowercase word 'oushy' set in a rounded geometric sans (Fredoka), ink grey, with the letter 'u' in orange and an 8-pointed hand-drawn tapered star sitting over it. The 8-pointed star with slightly organic, hand-drawn tapered points is the brand symbol and must keep that exact shape. Reproduce the wordmark and the star with the exact proportions of the reference, never redraw or restyle them. Design a seamless repeating pattern, square 1:1, on cream #F6EFE9. The motif is the brand 8-pointed hand-drawn star repeated on a regular grid of 6 columns by 6 rows, alternating three treatments in a checker rhythm: solid orange stars, solid ink-grey stars, and thin ink-grey outline stars. Each star is rotated slightly differently, between minus 15 and plus 15 degrees, so the grid reads hand-made rather than mechanical. Tiny orange small 8-pointed stars fill the gaps between rows. The pattern must tile: motifs touching the edges are cut and continue on the opposite side. Flat vector, no shadows, no text at all.
