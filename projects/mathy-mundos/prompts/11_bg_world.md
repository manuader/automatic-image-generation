## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the painting style, lighting, color treatment, level of detail and composition rules of the attached reference background - same game, same art team - but paint a DIFFERENT place, described below. Do not copy the objects of the reference.

Game background illustration for Mathy, a premium math puzzle game played by kids and adults alike. Style: stylized 2D digital painting with clean vector-like shapes and soft gradients, in the spirit of Monument Valley, Alto's Odyssey and premium mobile puzzle games. Twilight palette: deep teal and indigo sky, warm golden lantern lights, soft rim light, gentle atmospheric haze, a few fireflies. Calm, magical and inviting, never childish.

Scene: 'The World of Mathy'. A wide panoramic view of a chain of floating islands in a twilight sky, each island a different small world: an orchard, a river with stepping stones, an overgrown temple, a canyon workshop with gears, rolling hills with windmills, a lantern market, an observatory. A thin glowing golden trail winds from island to island, from the bottom left toward the top right. Here the center MAY have islands, but keep them small and far apart so text cards remain readable on top.

Composition, very important: wide 16:9 landscape image. No characters, no creatures, no people.

Color rules: keep the center low-contrast and slightly desaturated so the gameplay colors pop on top of it. Do not use bright red, bright mint green or bright amber anywhere near the center; those colors are reserved for the game. No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no UI, no watermark, no border, no frame.
