## Prompt

Game background illustration for Mathy, a premium math puzzle game played by kids and adults alike. Style: stylized 2D digital painting with clean vector-like shapes and soft gradients, in the spirit of Monument Valley, Alto's Odyssey and premium mobile puzzle games. Twilight palette: deep teal and indigo sky, warm golden lantern lights, soft rim light, gentle atmospheric haze, a few fireflies. Calm, magical and inviting, never childish.

Scene: 'The Orchard Terraces'. An orchard on old stone terraces at dusk. Round fruit trees with softly glowing fruits only on the far left and far right edges, small paper lanterns hanging from the branches, wooden crates of fruit near the edges, distant misty hills.

Composition, very important: wide 16:9 landscape image. The central area of the image, from 25 percent to 85 percent of the height and from 20 percent to 80 percent of the width, is a calm, dark, low-detail open ground seen slightly from above, with NOTHING on it: the game board will be drawn on top of it. All the detail lives on the left and right edges and in the sky. The horizon sits at about one third of the height. No characters, no creatures, no people.

Color rules: keep the center low-contrast and slightly desaturated so the gameplay colors pop on top of it. Do not use bright red, bright mint green or bright amber anywhere near the center; those colors are reserved for the game. No text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no UI, no watermark, no border, no frame.
